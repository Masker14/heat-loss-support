The figures are estimates

Heat Loss Calc is a quick aid for sizing radiators. Please understand what it is and is not:

  • Heat loss figures are rule-of-thumb estimates based on typical values for each kind of wall, window, floor and roof. They are deliberately on the high side. A real building may lose more or less heat than the estimate.
  • It is not a design calculation to BS EN 12831 or any other standard, and it must not be used where one is required, for example for a Microgeneration Certification Scheme (MCS) heat pump installation.
  • Radiator outputs are an average of manufacturers' published figures for generic panel types, not any one make. It is the designer's responsibility to check the manufacturer's data sheet for the actual radiator.
  • The app does not check pipe sizes, boiler or heat pump capacity, pump duty, hot water demand or any other part of a heating system.

You are responsible for the heating design and for checking that the result is suitable for the job. The app is intended for use by competent heating engineers and installers.
