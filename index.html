Common questions

How does it work out the heat loss?
For each room it adds up the heat lost through the outside walls, windows, doors, floor and ceiling, and the heat lost to air movement, for the temperature difference between the room and outside. Each choice you make (solid brick, old double glazing, loft with insulation) stands for a typical figure for that kind of construction. It then adds your margin, which is 10% unless you change it in Settings.

Why is the figure higher than another calculator's?
On purpose. Heat Loss Calc gives an estimate, and it is set to come out on the high side so that a room is not left cold. The result screen shows how much of the figure is your margin.

Is this a full heat loss calculation?
No. It is a quick estimate for sizing radiators, using typical figures for each kind of wall, window, floor and roof. Where a job needs a full room-by-room design calculation to a published standard, for example for a heat pump installation scheme, use a tool made for that.

Which radiators does it use?
Generic panel radiators: Type 11, 21 and 22, in the usual heights and standard lengths. The outputs are an average of manufacturers' published figures, not any one make, so always check the output of the actual radiator on its manufacturer's data sheet before you buy.

What does the system temperature setting do?
Radiator outputs in catalogues are quoted for a hot system (about 75°C flow). A system run cooler gives less heat from the same radiator. Choose standard boiler, boiler run cooler or 55° flow or heat pump in Settings and the app corrects every radiator's output to match.

The app says no standard size is big enough.
The room needs more heat than the longest radiator of that type and height gives. Choose a taller radiator or a Type 22, or tap 2 radiators and split the room between two.

How do I enter a bay, an alcove or an odd-shaped room?
Choose L shape or Other and split the room into rectangles. On each rectangle, tap only the walls that are outside walls. Where two rectangles join, leave that wall as inside.

Where are my jobs stored?
Only on your iPhone. Heat Loss Calc has no login and no server, so nobody else can see your jobs. They are included in your normal iPhone backup.

If I delete the app, are my jobs deleted?
Yes. Deleting Heat Loss Calc deletes every job in it, and we cannot get them back for you. Share the PDF of any job you want to keep before deleting the app.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Heat Loss Calc. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date, or before the end of the free trial, to avoid the next charge. After you cancel, your jobs can still be opened and their PDFs shared. Jobs more than 7 days old can no longer be changed.

Why can't I change one of my jobs?
Without a subscription, a job can be changed for 7 days after you create it. After that it can still be opened and its PDF shared, but not changed. Subscribing lets you change every job again.
