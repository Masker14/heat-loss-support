Privacy Policy

Heat Loss Calc for iPhone. Draft, not yet published.

In short: Heat Loss Calc collects no data. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you share a PDF yourself, and then it goes only where you send it.

What the app stores, and where

Each job's name, its rooms, their measurements and your choices, and your settings (including your company name, if you enter one) are stored on your iPhone only. There is no login, no server and no cloud copy run by us. Your jobs are included in your normal iPhone backup.

To count your free jobs, the app keeps a number in your iPhone's keychain, so reinstalling the app does not reset it. This number never leaves your iPhone.

Permissions

The app asks for none. It does not use your location, camera, photos, contacts or microphone.

Sharing a PDF

When you share a job's PDF, the app hands the file to the iPhone's share sheet and you choose where it goes (email, messages, files, print). It is sent by the app or service you choose, not by us, and we never receive a copy.

Subscriptions

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your name, Apple ID or payment details.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.
  • No data is sold or shared, because we receive none.

Deleting your data

Delete a room or a job inside the app, or delete the app to remove every job. PDFs you have already shared are kept by whoever received them.

Children

Heat Loss Calc is a work tool and is not directed at children.
